Privacy policy

Wishver · effective 18 November 2026

Wishver helps you remember birthdays and anniversaries and send wishes. It is built to keep your data on your phone. There is no Wishver server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

What stays on your phone

Everything you add is stored only on your phone: people's names, dates, phone numbers, relations, notes, gift ideas, photos, the wishes you sent and the days you marked as wished, and your settings. Your account (a password, kept only as a secure one-way hash, or the email of the Google account you signed in with) also stays on your phone.

Permissions, and what each is for

  • Contacts (optional): to find birthdays, fill in the people you add, and use their contact photos. Read on your phone only.
  • Calendar (optional): to import birthday and anniversary events. Read only; your calendar is never changed.
  • Photos (optional): only the pictures you pick, for a person or a memory. A small copy is kept inside the app.
  • Camera (optional): only to scan a Wishver QR code from another phone. No photos are taken or kept.
  • Microphone (optional): only while you speak a wish. Your phone's own speech service turns it into text.
  • Notifications, alarms and start-up: to show reminders on time, including after the phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint or Face ID (optional): to unlock the app. Wishver never sees your fingerprint or face.

Wishver tells you what each permission is for before your phone asks, and you can say no; the rest of the app keeps working.

When data leaves your phone, and only if you choose it

  • Google sign-in and backup. If you sign in with Google or connect Google Drive, Wishver backs up your list, history and (if you keep it on) photos to a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive, which only Wishver can read. Wishver asks only for your name, email and this app folder (scope drive.appdata); it cannot see your other Drive files, mail or contacts. You can disconnect in Settings, and delete the backup in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Wishver → Delete hidden app data.
  • AI wishes. If you turn on AI wishes with your own Google Gemini key, the details needed for a wish (first name, relation, occasion, and any note you added for that person) are sent to Google's Gemini service to write it. On phones with built-in AI, wishes are written on the phone and nothing is sent.
  • Sending wishes. When you tap Send, the wish opens in WhatsApp (or the app you pick) and you send it yourself.
  • Sharing and backups you make. Lists, cards and backup files are shared only where you send them.
  • Feedback. "Send feedback" and "Report a problem" open your email app; recent error messages kept on your phone are included only in a report you choose to send.

Wishver's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to back up and restore your own list, and is never transferred, sold or used for ads.

Deleting your data

Settings → Backup and sharing → Delete everything removes your list from the app; uninstalling removes everything on the phone. A Google Drive backup can be deleted as described above.

Children

Wishver is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
